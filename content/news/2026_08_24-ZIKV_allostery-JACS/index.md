Our paper on RNA-driven allostery of the ZIKV NS3 helicase has just been published in the Journal of the American Chemical Society!

<!--more-->
We use multiscale methods to reveal how RNA binding allosterically tunes the active site for ATP hydrolysis in the ZIKV NS3 helicase motor.
You can check out the full article here: https://pubs.acs.org/jacsat/article/doi/10.1021/jacs.6c11584/5323325/tuning-the-zikv-ns3-helicase-motor-activity
